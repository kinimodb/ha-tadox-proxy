# The Ki default goes back to 0.003; a lower Ki is a setting per room

Status: accepted for v1.6.0. Supersedes the `Ki` half of [ADR 0002](0002-integral-windup-low-gain-and-tight-floor.md); its integral floor of -0.5 °C stands. It takes the option that ADR 0002 rejected as "the floor alone" (`Ki` 0.003 with the floor of -0.5).

`Ki` defaults to 0.003 again, as up to v1.5.0; the pre-releases v1.6.0-beta.1 and beta.2 ran 0.001. The floor stays at -0.5 °C, the ceiling at +2.0 °C, `Kp` at 0.8 and the decay at 0.95. The config-entry migration to minor version 1.2 stays, but it only raises the version: an update never deletes or changes a value someone has stored.

## Context

- A three-day test of beta.2 in the maintainer's six rooms found rooms short of their target more often than on comparable days before. Mostly not because of the beta: reheats after the house had been empty or a door open, mornings when the boiler was probably busy with hot water, a living room at the limit of its radiator. The beta's own share was small: `Ki = 0.001` slowed the last tenth of a degree, in one clear case.
- The tester of #124, whose boiler fires on demand, refuted the beta's headline that the valve stays open only 1 to 9 minutes after the room reaches its target. That figure replayed his old error series, overshoot included, through the new regulator. In three real runs on beta.2 the proxy's logged `hvac_action` stayed `heating` after the crossing, so the setpoint it had sent stayed above the thermostat's reading: for at least 36 minutes (the recording ended), without going idle in the 94 minutes recorded, and for 16 minutes. A smaller overshoot leaves a smaller proportional part to cancel the integral. `Ki` did shrink the push above the target (58 → 17 and 10 → 3 K·min in the replay). His boiler is served by the needs-heat sensor, which follows `error_c` and does not depend on `Ki`.
- No default changes for everyone because of one setup; a value that can be set is tuned per room (TUNING.md). The floor cannot be set. The cold-side windup it stops shows in the maintainer's house, in its own data, and as a drift in the tester's run at the target on 27 Oct (the integral fell to -0.85 while the room sat just above its target with the valve shut). The floor never makes the integral lower than v1.5.0 would. So it stays.
- The betas' migration dropped a stored `Ki` of 0.003, also where someone had chosen it. Their release notes said so; Home Assistant itself showed no log entry and no message.

## Considered Options

Each option is named by its `Ki` and its floor.

- **0.001 / -0.5, the beta's numbers:** fewer commands and less valve time while warm in the models, but more minutes below target (656 / 644 a day in the closed-loop model, against 613 / 565 with this decision; see Consequences) and a slower last tenth; its gain for on-demand boilers did not hold.
- **0.003 / -2, both numbers back (v1.5.0):** the cold-side windup returns; the warm side is a little smaller than with this decision. Replaying the test days open loop through the real code, the six rooms together had the valve commanded shut while 0.1 K or more below target for 308 minutes a day, against 93 with this decision and 143 with the beta, and commanded open while 0.1 K or more above target for 123 minutes a day, against 153 with this decision and 55 with the beta.
- **0.001 / -2, only the floor back (ADR 0002's "`Ki` alone"):** the longest waits after a long warm stay; in the model a room 0.1 K below target after eight warm hours waited 304 minutes for its valve.
- **0.003 / -0.5, this decision (ADR 0002's "the floor alone"):** ADR 0002 rejected it because the integral then reaches its ceiling of +2 in two of the six rooms. Accepted now: the cold side is the best of these pairs, the warm side stays close to v1.5.0, a little more (open loop +24 %, closed-loop model +5 to 7 %), and a room that needs less push can run a lower `Ki` of its own.
- **0.002 / -0.5:** between 0.001 and 0.003 on both sides; the fallback if rooms at 0.003 overshoot clearly more than with v1.5.0. As a default it would reach only entries whose options dialog was never saved (see Consequences), so the fallback is a setting per room.
- **The tester's offer, a cap on the feedforward or the command at the target:** feedforward cancels out of the push, so only a cap on the push would act, and it would shut valves at the target where the maintainer's thermostats need a gap of 0.2 to 0.6 K to hold it (saw-tooth, colder, more commands). At most a later opt-in for on-demand boilers.
- **Later levers, model only:** a minimum push near the target (code), `Kp` 1.2 to 1.5 as a trial in one room.

## Consequences

- The warm side is close to v1.5.0, a little more: after a slow final approach the integral is three times what the beta left, about 1.2 °C after a last 0.3 °C that took 45 minutes, and the valve stays open past the target for a while (#124). Closed-loop model of four rooms in two simulators, per day, against v1.5.0: minutes more than 0.2 K below target 613 / 565 against 675 / 612; minutes with the valve commanded open while 0.1 K or more warm 145 / 136 against 135 / 129; commands 336 / 355 against 359 / 371. Tenths of a degree, not degrees. In the open-loop replay the integral reached its ceiling of +2 in two rooms, for 140 and 9 minutes in 2.97 days; with v1.5.0 its highest value was +1.78.
- A boiler that fires on demand follows the needs-heat sensor, which turns off once the room has reached its target, whatever the valve does. A room can also run a lower `Ki` of its own, for example 0.001: less push is left above the target, and the last tenth takes longer (TUNING.md). While the valve is open with no hot water behind it (`0 < error <` the switch-on value), the integral builds up as fast as with v1.5.0 again, because `Ki` is the same; ADR 0002's 0.86 K a day was simulated with `Ki = 0.001` and has not been simulated again.
- The migration keeps every stored value. An entry whose `Ki` a beta dropped follows the default again and runs 0.003. An entry whose options dialog was saved during a beta while `Ki` showed 0.001 holds 0.001 and keeps it until someone sets 0.003; an entry that held a value of its own then, for example 0.005, keeps that.
- The options dialog stores every field it shows, and no migration changes a stored value. So a later change of a default in that dialog reaches only entries whose dialog was never saved, and a change such as `Ki` 0.002 for rooms that already run is a setting per room.
- No number here was measured on the valve: the Tado X reports no valve position. The field figures (the maintainer's test, the tester's runs) are logged commands, readings and the proxy's own `hvac_action`; the rest come from models and replays of recorded inputs.
